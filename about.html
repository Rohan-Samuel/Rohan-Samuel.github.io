Hi, I'm Rohan

Unity & XR developer in Vancouver — open to gameplay, XR and technical audio roles.

I build interactive systems where the code and the sound are the same idea. On Harmony I composed every instrument layer and then wrote the Unity system that plays them; on Kinesthesia I did the same thing with a webcam and six floating squares; on Invasive I was lead programmer on a stealth game whose whole loop runs on an oxygen mechanic that pushes you toward the monsters. That combination — someone who can write the C# and produce the audio — is the thing I'm building a career around.

I studied at Simon Fraser University's School of Interactive Arts and Technology (SIAT), an interdisciplinary program that combines art and technology — which is where my interest in blending code, visuals, and sound really took shape. Since graduating, I've stayed close to that world: TA'ing and now lab-instructing XR development courses, while continuing to build my own VR installations, games, and short films.
