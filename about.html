Hi, I'm Rohan

With a deep interest in VR and XR, I believe technology and art should work in harmony with humans. I'm developing an expertise in programming, visual design, and audio production, and my work aims to push the limits of our imagination by combining these disciplines.

I studied at Simon Fraser University's School of Interactive Arts and Technology (SIAT), an interdisciplinary program that combines art and technology — which is where my interest in blending code, visuals, and sound really took shape. Since graduating, I've stayed close to that world: TA'ing and now lab-instructing XR development courses, while continuing to build my own VR installations, games, and short films.
