--- --- About — Rohan Samuel, Unity & XR Developer {% include nav.html %} {% include hero-about.html %}

Open to gameplay, XR, and technical audio roles.

I build interactive systems where the code, the sound, and the visuals are the same act. On Harmony I composed every instrument layer, textured the world, and then wrote the Unity system that plays them; on Kinesthesia I did the same thing with a webcam and six floating squares; on Invasive I was lead programmer on a stealth game whose whole loop runs on an oxygen mechanic that pushes you toward the monsters. That combination — a programmer's logic, a composer's ear, and a technical artist's eye — is the thing I'm building a career around.

I studied at Simon Fraser University's School of Interactive Arts and Technology (SIAT), an interdisciplinary program that combines art and technology — which is where my interest in blending code, visuals, and sound really took shape. Since graduating, I've stayed close to that world: TA'ing and then lab-instructing XR development courses, while continuing to build my own VR installations, games, and short films.
